Privacy Policy

How HelloChiwa collects, uses, and protects information.

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Introduction

HelloChiwa ("HelloChiwa", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to explaining clearly how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit or use HelloChiwa, including our language-learning reading content and related features.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use HelloChiwa, we may collect or process certain information.

Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect information required for account registration and authentication, such as your email address and authentication credentials.

Passwords are handled through our authentication service and are not intended to be stored by HelloChiwa in plain text.

Information You Provide

If you voluntarily provide information through features available on HelloChiwa, we may process that information for the purpose for which it was provided.

As additional features are introduced, such as comments, likes, profiles, or other interactive functions, the information required for those features may also be processed.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • create and maintain user accounts;
  • authenticate users and provide account access;
  • provide and improve HelloChiwa's reading and language-learning features;
  • maintain the security and functionality of the website;
  • respond to questions, feedback, or support requests;
  • understand how our services are used and improve the user experience;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the service without an appropriate legal basis or notice where required by applicable law.

3. Authentication and Supabase

HelloChiwa uses Supabase as a third-party service provider for authentication and related backend functionality.

When you create an account or sign in, information necessary for authentication may be processed by Supabase.

For more information about how Supabase handles data, users should review Supabase's own privacy documentation and policies.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

HelloChiwa may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies where necessary to operate the website, maintain sessions, remember preferences, improve functionality, or support analytics and advertising.

Some third-party services used on the website may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Where legally required, we will request appropriate consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

5. Advertising

HelloChiwa may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize advertising in accordance with their applicable policies and user choices.

Where required by applicable law, HelloChiwa will provide users with appropriate choices regarding consent and advertising personalization.

6. Third-Party Services

HelloChiwa may rely on third-party providers to operate certain parts of the service, including authentication, hosting, analytics, audio delivery, advertising, or other technical functionality.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information processed through HelloChiwa against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

9. Children's Privacy

HelloChiwa is not specifically designed as a service for young children.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information.

11. International Data Processing

Because HelloChiwa may use service providers located in different countries, information may be processed or stored outside your country of residence.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as HelloChiwa develops new features.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the contact information provided on the HelloChiwa website.
