Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using HelloChiwa.

1. General Questions

Is HelloChiwa free to use?

Yes, all our core reading materials, vocabulary explanations, and interactive features are 100% free to access.

How many languages are supported?

Currently, we offer reading materials and vocabulary support for 10 major languages, including Japanese, English, Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, and Russian.

2. Content & Learning

How often is new content added?

We regularly update our reading library with real-world articles, stories, and educational materials to ensure fresh practice content for language learners.

Do articles include audio pronunciations?

Yes, selected reading articles come with integrated audio support so learners can practice listening comprehension while reading the text.

3. Accounts & Privacy

Do I need an account to use HelloChiwa?

No account is required to browse and read articles. However, creating a free account allows you to save progress, bookmark favorite stories, and access personalized features.

How is my personal data protected?

We value your privacy and process user data securely using modern authentication standards (such as Supabase). For more details, please visit our Privacy Policy page.
